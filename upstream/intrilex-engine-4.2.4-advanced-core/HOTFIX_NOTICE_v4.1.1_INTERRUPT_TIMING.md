# Intrilex v4.1.1 — Interrupt Timing Hotfix

## Governing ruling

`Interrupt` is **timing authority only**.

- **Quick:** playable during your own Full Turn at a legal timing window.
- **Interrupt:** playable during any legal response window, including another player's Full Turn.
- Neither keyword inherently spends a Mini-Turn.
- Neither keyword inherently creates a Full-Turn skip, Action-Phase skip, or other turn tax.

## Explicit exceptions

- **10♠ — Stack Theft:** retains its printed Full-Turn skip consequences. If it resolves, both the interceptor and original caster receive the printed skip. If 10♠ is countered, only its controller receives the printed skip.
- **Time Bomb Defuse:** retains its separately printed Action-Phase skip whether it resolves or is countered.

## Removed inherited penalties

- Courageous Assault
- Rejuvenation
- Red Nine — Goal Shock

This hotfix supersedes every v4.1.0 sentence or fixture that treated the `Interrupt` keyword itself as a generic penalty source. All unrelated v4.1 rules remain unchanged.
