# Known Limitations

1. `core-advanced-authority` is bounded Advanced Core, not unrestricted complete Core.
2. Hidden Super branches (⭐3, ⭐5, ⭐6, ⭐7), generated effect copy, and Sudden Death remain replay-only. 10♦ Mimic, 10♣ Foundation, ⭐2 Hold, Voltage 3/4/5 Refine, and special scoring riders are now fully implemented.
3. Optional modules and multiplayer remain blocked by the strategic freeze.
4. Full state rewind is exact at command checkpoints, not after every emitted event.
5. Only 12 retained matches include full semantic fact detail; other campaign matches retain summaries and selected replay pairs.
6. The 100-match real cohort validates integration and evidence plumbing, not broad balance conclusions.
7. Pair estimates with low joint opportunity are marked insufficient; many are not discoveries.
8. The 10,800-row responsiveness benchmark repeats certified summaries to test scale and is not additional game evidence.
9. Chromium accessibility-tree and automated responsive checks do not replace independent manual assistive-technology testing.
10. The shipped policies are deterministic behavioral baselines, not optimal agents.
