# Causal and Synergy Model

The release records declaration, resolution, state-delta, and causal-edge facts. Edges include declared, responded-to, countered, generated, triggered, replaced, resolved-into, and caused-state-delta.

A relationship is not labeled synergy from co-occurrence alone. The estimator compares stratified joint-use outcomes against component-only baselines, requires joint opportunity/use counts, applies deterministic clustered uncertainty, BH correction, and shrinkage, and links representative and contradictory retained matches.

Current evidence contains **221** pair estimates and **60** recurring motifs. Low-sample estimates remain visible but are marked insufficient and suppressed from discovery ranking.

The model supports analytical turning-point labels only as decomposed associations. It does not claim perfect play, solved strategy, or causal certainty.
