Intrilex Card Art Pack

Contents:
- 54 standalone PNG artworks named for card codes.
- Naming scheme:
  - Suited cards: [rank][suit].png
    - ranks: a,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10,j,q,k
    - suits: s=Spades, h=Hearts, d=Diamonds, c=Clubs
    - examples: as.png, 10d.png, qc.png
  - Jokers: joker1.png, joker2.png

Notes:
- These are final standalone art assets with no card rules/text baked in.
- File naming is packaged for direct AI-agent / renderer ingestion.
- Mapping is best-effort based on the generated production batches in this chat.
