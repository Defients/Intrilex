## Post-repair discipline

After the v0.28.1 rules-parity hotfix lands, this entire report chain becomes
a **historical before-state**. It must not be regenerated in-place. New
evidence belongs to a separate post-repair corpus (see roadmap Phase 2 —
v0.28.2: Evidence Recalibration). Pre-fix and post-fix evidence must never be
silently combined.
